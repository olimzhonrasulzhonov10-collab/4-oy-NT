1.width, max-width, height
Vazifa pastdagi kartaga width:100%,max-width:300px va height:150px qiymatini bering. Brauzer oynasini kichraytirib-kattalashtirib, kartaning qanday o'zgarishini kuzating.
Bu karta o'lchami ozgartiring

2. object-fit
Vazifa: Pastdagi 4ta rasmga navbat bilan object-fit:cover, contain, fill va none qiymatini bering.Har birida rasm qanday joylashishini solishtiring.

3. object-position
Vazifa: Pastdagi rasmga object-position xossasini qo'shin (masalan : top,bottom yoki 20%,80%).Rasmning qaysi qismi ko'rinishini o'zgartirib ko'ring.
